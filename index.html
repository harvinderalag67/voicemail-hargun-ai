How to Publish as a Mobile App

Architecture Roadmap

Step-by-step guidance for iOS App Store, Google Play, and PWA distribution

✓ Already Implemented in this Project: VoiceFlow AI already has a valid Web App Manifest (/manifest.webmanifest), Service Worker (/sw.js), Apple Touch Icons, and offline caching configured!

Why Start with a PWA?

  • Zero App Store Approval Delays: Publish updates instantaneously to users' home screens without waiting 24–72 hours for Apple/Google app reviewers.
  • 0% Platform Cut: Keep 100% of your $39 Lifetime Pass revenue (no 15% to 30% App Store in-app purchase commissions).
  • Native Standalone Experience: Launches directly from home screen icons without Safari or Chrome browser navigation chrome.
Deploy to Vercel Drop or Netlify in 10 seconds: 100% Static HTML

Drag and drop your project directory into vercel.com/new or GitHub. When users visit your URL on their phone, they can install it with 1 click.

To publish on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store under your company name, the gold-standard framework is Capacitor by Ionic. It wraps this exact HTML/JS codebase into real Xcode & Android Studio projects with zero rewrite needed!

Terminal CLI Commands: 
# 1. Install Capacitor in your repo
npm install @capacitor/core @capacitor/cli

# 2. Initialize Capacitor (capacitor.config.json is pre-created in root)
npx cap init "VoiceFlow AI" "com.voiceflow.ai" --web-dir .

# 3. Add Native iOS and Android platforms
npm install @capacitor/ios @capacitor/android
npx cap add ios
npx cap add android

# 4. Open Xcode for iOS build & TestFlight
npx cap open ios

# 5. Open Android Studio for Google Play .aab
npx cap open android
Important: iOS Microphone Permissions (Info.plist)

Apple requires clear disclosure for audio recording. In your Xcode Info.plist, add: 

<key>NSMicrophoneUsageDescription</key>
<string>VoiceFlow needs microphone access to convert your spoken ideas into prioritized action items.</string>

If your main goal is getting on the Google Play Store in less than 30 minutes, Microsoft's PWABuilder uses Google's Trusted Web Activity (TWA) to package your live PWA into a signed Android App Bundle (.aab) without even installing Android Studio.

  1. Deploy VoiceFlow to your custom domain or Vercel URL with HTTPS.
  2. Go to PWABuilder.com and enter your URL.
  3. Click "Package for Store" and select Android.
  4. Download the generated voiceflow-release.aab bundle.
  5. Upload this file directly to the Google Play Console under your Google Developer account ($25 one-time registration).
